# Albert Slide Deck Builder

## Required Context

Albert's deck standards are the Design Standards section below. They are the complete preference set. If Albert attaches or names a preferences document in the session, apply it on top of these standards. Ask Albert only when a design or messaging choice is genuinely his call.

## Workflow

1. Clarify the audience, decision, venue, format, length, and source materials when those are unclear.
2. Use the presentation or Google Slides skill/tooling that matches the target file type.
3. Build the narrative before polishing slides. Make each slide earn its place.
4. Use `albert-writing-style` for text that appears in the deck.
5. Use direct, credible visuals. Avoid decorative filler, generic icons, and overbuilt layouts.
6. Render or inspect the deck before delivery. Check layout, fit, alignment, chart readability, title logic, and speaker flow.
7. Iterate until the deck passes visual QA or the remaining issue needs Albert's call.

## Design Standards

- Use restrained executive styling for SaaS, CRM, operational, AI strategy, customer, or board-style work.
- Prioritize scanability, hierarchy, and credibility over ornament.
- Keep titles useful. They should state the point of the slide, not just the topic.
- Use diagrams only when they clarify relationships, flow, ownership, timing, or tradeoffs.
- Make tables and charts readable at presentation distance.
- Keep text short enough to fit cleanly without crowding.
