# QA Checklist

Run every deliverable through this before returning it. Fix what fails; note anything you can't fix.

## Accuracy & Citations
- [ ] Every factual claim (stat, trend, quote, competitor detail) has a traceable source.
- [ ] No claim is stated as fact when it's actually an estimate or projection — those are labeled as such.
- [ ] Sources are current enough to be trustworthy for the claim being made.

## Brand Voice Alignment
- [ ] Tone matches the brand voice loaded in Stage 0.
- [ ] No vocabulary or phrasing the brand explicitly avoids.
- [ ] Consistent voice across all pieces in the table, not just the first one.

## Audience Fit
- [ ] Content speaks to the stated audience's actual questions or stage of awareness.
- [ ] Channel-native format (a LinkedIn post isn't just a tweet with more words).
- [ ] No jargon or assumed context the audience wouldn't have.

## Actionability
- [ ] Every plan row maps back to a stated goal or channel in the brief — nothing orphaned, nothing from the brief missing.
- [ ] Each piece has enough detail (draft copy, date, channel, owner) that someone could execute it without asking follow-up questions.
- [ ] Timeline/dates are internally consistent (no overlapping conflicts, no gaps against the stated cadence).

## Formatting
- [ ] Delivered as a table, not prose paragraphs.
- [ ] Table includes: identifier, channel, draft/summary, source/citation, status-notes column.
- [ ] Assumptions made without confirmation are flagged, either in a notes column or a short list above the table.
