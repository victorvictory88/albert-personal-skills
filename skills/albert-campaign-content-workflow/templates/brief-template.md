# Content Brief

**Request type:** Campaign / Content calendar / Repurposing (pick one)

## Goal
What outcome should this content produce? (awareness, leads, signups, engagement, retention)

## Audience
Who is this for? Include persona detail if known — role, stage of awareness, channel habits.

## Brand voice
Tone, vocabulary, and anything the brand explicitly avoids saying. Source: (file path, prior conversation, or "assumed — confirm").

## Channels
Where will this run? (LinkedIn, X, email, blog, Instagram, etc.)

## Timeline
Start date, end date, key milestone dates (launch day, event date, publish cadence).

## Source material
For repurposing: the asset being adapted (link, file, transcript). For campaigns/calendars: any existing assets to build from or coordinate with.

## Constraints
Budget, legal/compliance limits, word count or format limits, approval steps.

## Success metrics
How will this be judged? (engagement rate, clicks, conversions, qualitative fit)

## Assumptions made
List anything filled in without explicit confirmation from the requester, so it's easy to flag in review.
